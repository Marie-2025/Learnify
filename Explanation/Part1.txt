### **1. `App.js` – The "Brain" of Navigation**  
Think of `App.js` as the **traffic controller** of your app. It decides where each screen is and how users move between them.  

- We use **React Navigation** to create a **Stack Navigator** (a stack is like a deck of cards; you move from one card to the next).  
- The `<NavigationContainer>` is like a **bus station**—it holds all the screens and lets us move between them.  
- `<Stack.Navigator>` is like a **bus route**—it lists the screens (stops) where users can go.  
- `<Stack.Screen name="Welcome" component={WelcomeScreen} />` is one of the **bus stops**—when the app starts, this screen appears first.  

#### **Example Analogy**  
Imagine your app is a **city**, and each screen is a **bus stop**. The **Stack Navigator** is the **bus route**, and users can move from one stop (screen) to another. 🚍  

---

### **2. `WelcomeScreen.js` – The Introductory Billboard**  
This screen is like a **big welcome billboard** on a road, showing:  
✅ A **logo** (image)  
✅ A **welcome message**  
✅ A **tagline**  
✅ After 3 seconds, it **automatically moves to the next screen** (Page1.js).  

#### **Key Parts Explained**  
- `useEffect(() => { setTimeout(() => { navigation.replace('Page1'); }, 3000); }, []);`  
  - This is like setting an **alarm**⏳. After 3 seconds, the screen automatically **moves to Page1.js**.  
  - `navigation.replace('Page1')` means **go to Page1 and don’t come back** (it removes the Welcome Screen from history).  

- `<Image source={require('../assets/logo.png')} style={styles.logo} />`  
  - This **displays your logo**. The image file should be inside the `assets/` folder.  

- `<Text>`  
  - We use `<Text>` to show words like **"Welcome to Learnify!"** and **"Learn Anytime, Anywhere"**.  

#### **Example Analogy**  
Think of this screen as a **cinema intro scene**. When you start a movie, you see a **studio logo** (like "Marvel" or "Disney"), a **welcome message**, and then the movie starts (navigates to the next screen). 🎬  

---

### **3. `Page1.js` – The Next Chapter**  
Once the Welcome Screen disappears, users land on **Page1.js**.  
This is just a placeholder for now, but later we can make it a **login screen, homepage, or course list**.  

It simply shows:  
✅ **A title: "This is Page 1"**  

#### **Example Analogy**  
Imagine opening a **book** 📖. The **Welcome Screen** is the **book cover**, and **Page1.js** is the **first chapter**.  

---

### **4. `styles` – The "Clothes" of the App**  
Every screen has **styles** to control its look (color, size, spacing).  

- `flex: 1` → Makes sure the screen **fills** the entire space.  
- `justifyContent: 'center'` → Centers everything **vertically**.  
- `alignItems: 'center'` → Centers everything **horizontally**.  
- `backgroundColor: '#f5f5f5'` → Changes the screen’s **background color**.  

#### **Example Analogy**  
If the app is a **person**, styles are its **clothes** 👕. Without them, it would be **plain and boring**.  

---

### **How Everything Works Together**  
1️⃣ `App.js` **loads** the Welcome Screen first.  
2️⃣ `WelcomeScreen.js` **shows a logo, text, and tagline** for 3 seconds.  
3️⃣ After 3 seconds, it **automatically moves to Page1.js**.  
4️⃣ `Page1.js` **displays a simple message for now**.  

---

### **Final Example Analogy – A Movie Premiere 🎬**  
- `App.js` → The **cinema** where everything happens.  
- `WelcomeScreen.js` → The **studio logo intro** (Marvel, Disney).  
- `setTimeout()` → The **countdown** before the movie starts.  
- `Page1.js` → The **first scene of the movie**.
